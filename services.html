Services

Start with the surface you have. A worn tennis court, an asphalt driveway, and a concrete garage floor each need different preparation and a different finish.

Options by surface

Tennis court restoration

We start by looking for cracks, worn areas, and faded lines. Resurfacing can include crack repair, new court coatings, and fresh lines. The same planning applies to basketball and pickleball courts.

Asphalt sealcoating

Driveways and parking lots need a clean surface before a sealcoat goes down. Crack filling can address smaller openings first. Parking lot work can also include repainting the lines.

Epoxy floor coatings

Garage and work-area floors need clean, sound concrete. Surface preparation helps the coating bond, and a smooth finish makes the floor easier to sweep and wipe down.
